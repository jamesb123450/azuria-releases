Friends and messaging

Your friends.
A little closer.

Connect your Azuria account, add friends by username and keep your conversations together in Social. Switch between Friends and Chats, search your list and pick up where you left off.

How friends and messaging work

Private conversations, simple controls.

  • Send friend requests and chat with accepted friends
  • See unread messages and load earlier conversations
  • Manage requests, remove friends or block an account

New messages are end-to-end encrypted. Save an encrypted recovery file to restore your messaging key on another Windows PC.

Older messages sent before encryption remain unencrypted and are labelled in the launcher. Your Azuria account is separate from the Microsoft account used to play Minecraft.
