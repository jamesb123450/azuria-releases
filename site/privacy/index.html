Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. How Azuria handles information when you visit this website or use the launcher.

About this policyInformation we useWebsite & cookiesService providersStorage & retentionYour rightsChanges & contact

1. About this policy

Azuria Launcher is an independent Minecraft Java launcher developed and maintained by Azuria Studios. In this policy, “Azuria”, “we” and “our” mean Azuria Studios. Azuria is not affiliated with Microsoft or Mojang.

This policy covers the Azuria website, launcher and project-operated content service. Third-party services have their own policies. Read this alongside our terms of service.

2. Information we use and why

Launcher data. Preferences, Minecraft profile information, instances, skins, screenshots and game logs are stored on your computer to provide launcher features. Saved sign-in credentials are protected using Windows Credential Manager. Azuria does not receive or store your Microsoft password.

Accounts and downloads. Microsoft, Xbox and Minecraft Services authenticate your account, check game ownership and provide your profile, skins and capes. Update, game-file and community-content requests include connection information, such as your IP address, and relevant searches, versions or content identifiers to deliver results and downloads.

Azuria friends and messages. We store your friend connections, blocks, encrypted message contents, public encryption keys, sender and recipient account IDs, send times and read times in Supabase. New messages are end-to-end encrypted in the launcher. Private keys stay in Windows Credential Manager. Recovery files are encrypted with a separate passphrase and saved where you choose. Older messages sent before encryption remain unencrypted and are labelled in the app. We cannot recover encrypted messages if you lose your key and its recovery file. Removing or blocking a friend stops conversation access; adding them again restores its history. Messages remain until an account involved is deleted or you ask us to delete the data. We do not share your email with friends.

Support. If you contact the project, we receive your username, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate problems and maintain the project. GitHub issues are public; do not post credentials or private personal information. The contact page does not currently submit messages.

Azuria does not include launcher analytics, automatically upload your game logs, sell personal information or include targeted advertising in the launcher.

3. Website, cookies and advertising

When configured, we use Google Analytics to understand page visits, device and browser information, and how the website is used. The Google tag loads only after you accept analytics. Google may use first-party analytics cookies and process technical information, including your IP address. Advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled in our tag configuration. We omit query strings and fragments from the page address sent with the initial page view.

Use Cookie settings in the footer to accept, reject or withdraw analytics consent. Your choice is stored locally for up to 180 days, separately from any advertising choices. Changing the analytics measurement ID requires a new choice. Rejecting analytics prevents the tag from loading; withdrawing consent stops it and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies. Google may process data outside the UK under its applicable safeguards. See Google’s privacy policy for its handling and retention practices. We do not send account details or launcher data to website analytics.

This website does not provide user accounts. Fonts and interface images are hosted with the website. News articles and their cover images are served through Supabase, which receives technical request information to deliver that content. Admin usernames and publication dates are shown publicly with their stories. Hosting and download providers process technical request information, including IP addresses, browser details and requested pages, to deliver and protect their services. The main website uses Vercel; copies served through GitHub Pages and downloads use GitHub. Your browser also requests the latest published release from GitHub’s public API.

This website has no embedded social feeds. Optional advertising must be disclosed and the required advertising consent controls provided before it is enabled.

4. Service providers and sharing

Azuria connects to Microsoft/Mojang, GitHub, selected mod-loader services, Modrinth, CurseForge and their download providers as needed for the features you use. CurseForge catalogue requests may pass through Azuria’s Cloudflare-hosted service to forward searches and content requests and prevent abuse. Microsoft tokens and game logs are not sent to this service.

Optional Discord activity sends a generic Playing Minecraft status and start time to your local Discord client. Account, instance, world and server names are not included. You can turn this off in Azuria’s Privacy or Behaviour settings or Discord’s activity controls.

Providers may process information outside your country. Their own policies explain their processing, retention and international-transfer arrangements. Information shared with the project is used for the purposes described here; we may also disclose it where required by law.

5. Storage, retention and legal bases

Local launcher files remain until you remove them. Signing out in Profile removes saved sign-in credentials. Uninstalling does not automatically delete instances, worlds, screenshots, logs, local trash or backups. Back up important files before deleting them.

Support information is kept while needed to resolve enquiries, maintain the project or meet legal obligations. Public issue history may remain available through GitHub, and copies may exist outside our control. Providers retain their own request and security records under their policies.

Where data protection law applies, we rely on legitimate interests in responding to support requests and operating secure, reliable project services. Where optional website advertising requires consent, that processing is based on your consent. Other providers explain their own legal bases in their policies.

6. Your choices and rights

GDPR and UK GDPR

Where applicable, we handle personal information under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK GDPR and the UK Data Protection Act 2018. This includes using a lawful basis for processing and respecting the rights described below. Optional website analytics is based on your consent. You can withdraw consent using Cookie settings without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

You can browse without signing in. Launcher privacy settings control account reconnection, visible account details and optional Discord activity. First installation asks you to acknowledge the policy; automatic updates do not ask again. Acknowledgement is not consent to advertising, analytics or unrelated collection.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of personal information held by the project, or object to its processing. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. We may need to verify your identity to handle a request. For information held independently by Microsoft or another provider, use that provider’s privacy process.

Use the contact page to arrange a suitable route for a request without publishing private details. You may complain to your local data protection authority; UK users can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office. Parents or guardians with concerns about a child’s information can use the same contact route.

7. Changes and contact

We will publish revisions here with an updated date. Material changes to how personal information is used will be communicated before the new processing starts. A policy update does not itself give permission for new optional collection.

For questions about this policy, visit Contact Azuria.
