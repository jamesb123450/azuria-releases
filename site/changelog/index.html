v0.2.233

21 October 2026
Latest

A smoother launcher library

- Browse Minecraft versions and installed modpacks in matching portrait cards. - Select a card to update its launch panel; use the gear to open that profile. - Open the game monitor before launching, with live preparation progress. - Manage your Minecraft account from the new dropdown menu. - Smoother page, menu and selection transitions respect reduced-motion settings.

Easier Azuria sign-in

- Return from your browser to Azuria Launcher to connect your account automatically. - Use the Open Azuria Launcher button if your browser blocks the return prompt. - Manual connection codes remain available as a fallback. - Cancelling sign-in invalidates the pending connection request.
