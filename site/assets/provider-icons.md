# Provider icons

Icons identify the content providers; they do not imply endorsement of Azuria.
The original vector geometry is retained, with each icon rendered in its brand colour.

- `modrinth.svg`: Modrinth’s public website navigation icon, https://modrinth.com/ (retrieved 19 October 2026). Modrinth branding belongs to Rinth, Inc.
- `curseforge.svg`: `site-logo-mobile` from https://www.curseforge.com/images/sprite.svg (retrieved 19 October 2026). CurseForge branding belongs to its respective owner.

These brand assets are not covered by Azuria’s source-code licence.
